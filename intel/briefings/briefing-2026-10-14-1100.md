- **Component:** `wireguard` (Target: `nixos`)
- **Base CVSS Score:** `8.5` (`HIGH [P1]`)
- **Published At:** `2026-10-14 08:00:00 UTC`

**Threat Vector & Technical Impact:**
Embedding unencrypted private keys in NixOS configuration files exposes credentials via world-readable /nix/store paths, while unverified allowedIPs subnets permit inter-peer traffic interception.

**Mitigation & Hardening Playbook:**
